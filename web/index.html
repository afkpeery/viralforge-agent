What “connected” means

Only your approved account information is retrieved through official APIs. OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest on this server. No scraping or account impersonation. Neither service shares its proprietary feed-ranking model through these APIs.

Campaign and variant tracking

A separate conversion algorithm scores click-through and post-click outcomes, rather than optimizing for empty views.

For production, send conversions from your trusted backend (e.g. checkout webhook) with the secret header. Don't expose the secret key in client-side tracking scripts.

Conversion experiment output

CTR and conversion-rate estimates are smoothed with conservative priors to reduce overconfidence from tiny samples.
